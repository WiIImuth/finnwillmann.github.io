## What this site does not do

This site consists of static files. There is no login, no comment section and no
contact form. No cookies are set, no analytics or tracking services are
included, no fonts or scripts are loaded from third party servers, and no
profile is built about you. The light and dark switch stores your choice inside
your own browser only, that value never leaves your device.

## Hosting and server logs

The site is delivered through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc.,
88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, a Microsoft company.

When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary access data,
including your IP address, date and time, the requested file, the amount of data
transferred and details about your browser and operating system. That processing
is required for the page to reach you at all and serves the security and
stability of the service. The legal basis is legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)
(f) GDPR.

Data is transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub commits to the standard
contractual clauses of the EU Commission for that transfer. I have no influence
on how long these logs are kept, and I do not receive any evaluation of them.
More detail is available in GitHub's privacy statement at
[docs.github.com](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Contact by email

If you write to me, I process your message and your sender address in order to
answer it. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. I delete such messages
once they are dealt with and no retention obligation applies.

## Outbound links

The site links to Instagram and itch.io. Those services only learn something
about you once you click such a link and their page loads. No content from these
providers is embedded directly. Their own privacy notices apply to whatever they
process.
