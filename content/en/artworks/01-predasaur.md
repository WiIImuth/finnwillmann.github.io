A vehicle concept that takes a predatory dinosaur as its model. The idea is
already in the name, and the silhouette picks it up: a crouched stance, teeth
across the front, armour plates over the wheels.

It came out of the Game Art module. It started with concept drawings, which the
model in Blender was built from. The clay shot shows the pure form without
material, the beauty shot the same form with textures, neon lines and lighting.
The video next to them is a turntable that rotates the model once around its
own axis.
