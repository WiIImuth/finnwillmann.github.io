Exercise sheets from my design fundamentals course. Not a project with a
beginning and an end, but what comes out of drawing regularly: hands, a skull,
construction and perspective, and in between a character that was simply fun to
draw.

The effort behind them varies. Some sheets are ten minute sketches, others took
an afternoon. Both belong here, because without the quick ones the slow ones
would not exist.
