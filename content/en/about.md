I study at Hochschule Macromedia and build games in Unity. The range goes from
a seven day jam in a team of three to a solo exam project where I handled
everything myself: concept, code, levels, visual design.

The thread running through all of it is game feel. For GrappleGlory I did not
build the courses by eye. I calculated them against the controller's actual
physics values and rebuilt the momentum system until grapple, wallrun and jump
chain together without losing speed. Details like that get me further than long
feature lists.

Working in a way others can follow matters to me almost as much. Every project
comes with a log: problem, solution, reasoning. Wrong diagnoses stay in there
and are marked as such. That has saved me hours more than once. Above all it
taught me not to trust a plausible explanation until it accounts for every
symptom, not just the loudest one.

## Tools

Unity and C# are home. Blender for 3D assets, custom shaders, and editor tools
wherever work repeats and needs to become reproducible. I do sound myself as
well. In GrappleGlory the interface sounds are synthesised at runtime rather
than played back from files.
